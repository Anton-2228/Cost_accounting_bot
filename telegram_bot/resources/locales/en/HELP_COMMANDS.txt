Commands

/menu — menu: spreadsheet link, sync, sharing by email, unlinking, settings
/help — this help
/start — entry point; it also interrupts a dialog in progress

/add currency amount category note
    Example: /add euros 20 food coffee to go
/del id
    Without an id, I'll delete the last transaction of the period.
    Before deleting, I'll show the transaction and ask you to type its id.

/check — process a receipt scanned in the app
    I show the items and suggest types and categories.
    To correct: lines like “1,3 - dairy”.
    Postpone the receipt, delete it or leave processing — with the buttons under the message.

Everything you do with the spreadsheet itself is in /menu.

While I'm creating the spreadsheet, nothing works except /start and /help: there is nothing to look at yet. I'll send a message as soon as it's ready.

Write the amount without a sign: I tell income from expenses by the category.
You can write the fractional part with either a dot or a comma.

The currency is required and comes first:
/add euros 20 food coffee
Write it as you like: rubles, dollars, euros, dinars — or short: rub, usd, eur, rsd.
The currency of a receipt isn't asked: I know it from the receipt itself.
